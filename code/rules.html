Drag Race 3D

Quick Start Guide

Core Mechanics

Race down a 400-meter straight. Launch and shift gears at the right moment to cover the distance in the shortest time.

  • Shift Window (Green Zone): Upshift when the tachometer needle is inside the green zone to maintain peak acceleration.
  • Clutch Boost: Shifting exactly at the target RPM grants a brief torque surge.
  • Rev Limiter: Hitting the absolute redline activates fuel cut-off, braking your car.
  • Penalties & Tire Smoke: Shifting too early or too late reduces engine torque and activates wheel braking — thick grey smoke appears behind the rear wheels.

Solo Mode

Desktop Controls
  • W : Gas / Throttle (during countdown only)
  • D : Gear Up (once race starts)
Mobile Controls
  • Hold Screen : Rev the engine during countdown
  • Tap Screen : Gear Up during race
The Launch

During the 5-second countdown, hold throttle to keep the RPM needle inside the green Launch Window. Nailing it at GO! grants full grip from the start. Missing it causes a heavy penalty.

Car Selection

Player 1
  • A / D : Navigate cars
  • S : Select / Cancel
  • Mobile: Swipe left/right · Tap button
Player 2 (Multiplayer only)
  • ← → : Navigate cars
  • Enter : Select / Cancel
  • Mobile: Swipe left/right · Tap button

Multiplayer Mode

Head-to-head split-screen racing on a single keyboard or touch screen.

Player 1 (Left Side)
  • W : Gas / Throttle
  • D : Gear Up
  • Mobile: Hold/Tap Left Half of Screen
Player 2 (Right Side)
  • ↑ Arrow Up : Gas / Throttle
  • → Arrow Right : Gear Up
  • Mobile: Hold/Tap Right Half of Screen

Dynamic Camera & Police Lights: In multiplayer, the camera FOV auto-adjusts based on the gap between players. Blue and red police strobes follow the action throughout the race.
