Drag Race 3D

Quick Start Guide

Core Mechanics

Race down a 400-meter straight. Launch and shift gears perfectly to cover the distance in the shortest time.

  • Shift Window (Green Zone): Upshift when the tachometer needle is inside the green zone to maintain peak acceleration.
  • Clutch Boost: Shifting perfectly right above the target target grants a brief horsepower surge.
  • Rev Limiter: Don't hit the absolute redline limit! Fuel cut-off will activate, braking your car.
  • Friction Penalties & Tire Smoke: Shifting too early or late reduces engine torque and activates active wheel brakes. This instantly spawns **thick gray smoke** behind your rear wheels.

Solo Mode

Controls
  • W or ↑ Arrow Up : Gas / Throttle
  • D or → Arrow Right : Gear Up
  • A or ← Arrow Left : Gear Down
The Launch

During the 5s countdown, rev the engine to keep the RPM needle inside the green Launch Window. Landing inside it at "GO!" grants instant, full grip. Missing it severely drags your launch.

Multiplayer Mode

Head-to-head split-screen racing on a single keyboard.

Player 1 (Left Screen)
  • W : Gas / Throttle
  • D : Gear Up
  • A : Gear Down
Player 2 (Right Screen)
  • ↑ Arrow Up : Gas / Throttle
  • → Arrow Right : Gear Up
  • ← Arrow Left : Gear Down

Dynamic Camera & Police: The camera auto-adjusts its Field of View (FOV) based on the distance between players to maximize high-speed drama. Watch out for the blue and red flashing lights on the starting grid!
